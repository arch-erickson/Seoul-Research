# Seoul Research

**Seoul: The Carrot and the Stick.** A presentation for ARCH 3551 Sustainability History & Practice at CUNY City Tech. It compares how Seoul and New York deal with buildings, energy, waste, green space and transit, and what New York could learn from Seoul.

**View the presentation:** https://arch-erickson.github.io/Seoul-Research/

## Presenting

Open the link in Chrome or Edge, then press **P**. If a projector or second screen is connected, the slides go full screen there and the presenter view (current slide, next slide, notes and timers) opens on your laptop. On one screen, **P** goes full screen.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right arrow, Space, Enter, Page Down | Next step or next slide |
| Left arrow, Page Up, Backspace | Back. Going back to an earlier slide pauses the timer, and the next press only resumes it |
| T | Pause or resume the timer |
| A | Turn timed reveals on or off |
| P / Shift+P | Present on the projector / open the presenter view |
| B | Black screen |
| F | Full screen |
| G, a number, Enter | Jump to a slide |
